# The Mutopia files triage cached here were three different pieces

Triage matched "The Man I Love" against three Mutopia LilyPond files that turned
out to be Peter Lutkin's *The Lord Bless You and Keep You* (1900, twice) and
C. W. Glover's *Do They Think of Me At Home?* (c.1851). The matcher hit on
"dlib.indiana.edu" and "Indiana Edu. Web" in their metadata fields, which is also
how it mis-cached the Indiana slug.

Deleted, because a wrong cache is worse than an empty one: it is an instruction
to transcribe the wrong thing. Note that `src/sources.py probe` will find them
again if it is re-run on this slug.

The song itself was written from the 1924 Harms first edition.
